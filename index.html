About the Event

Welcome to the IP Paris – Hi! PARIS 2026 Computer Vision Workshop, a collaborative event bringing together experts, students, and professionals in the field of computer vision.

Hosted at Télécom Paris, this workshop aims to explore the latest research breakthroughs, share insights, and foster discussions among participants.
